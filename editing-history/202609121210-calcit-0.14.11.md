# Calcit 0.14.11 / Calcit 0.14.11

## 中文

- 将项目要求的 Calcit 版本从 0.13.58 升级到 0.14.11，保持 DBT 无损 `Buffer` 边界与模块版本不变。
- 结构化的 `Fn` schema 取代整份 `Dynamic`；`--check-only`、质量门禁与 dynamic-method 门禁通过，原生 dylib smoke 通过。
- 将 CI 的 `actions/checkout`、`calcit-lang/setup-calcit`、`Swatinem/rust-cache` 固定到不可变 commit。

## English

- Upgrade the required Calcit version from 0.13.58 to 0.14.11 while preserving the DBT lossless `Buffer` boundary and module version.
- Structured `Fn` schemas replace whole-`Dynamic`; `--check-only`, the quality gate, and the dynamic-method gate pass, and the native dylib smoke passes.
- Pin `actions/checkout`, `calcit-lang/setup-calcit`, and `Swatinem/rust-cache` in CI to immutable commits.
